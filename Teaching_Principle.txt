MY TEACHING PRINCIPLE
I believe that teaching is not just about sharing knowledge but also about understanding, guiding, and inspiring learners. Every student has different abilities, interests, and ways of understanding. As a future educator, I believe that patience, kindness, flexibility, and understanding are important in creating a positive learning environment where everyone feels accepted and to maintain inclusion.
I believe that learning becomes more meaningful when students are actively involved in the lesson. Instead of relying only on discussions, I will use activities, demonstrations, and appropriate instructional materials to make lessons more engaging and easier to understand. A good teacher should also be willing to learn, improve, and adapt to the needs of their students.
My principle is to teach with knowledge, lead with kindness, and inspire through understanding. I want to become a teacher who does not only educate minds but also encourages students to believe in themselves and discover their potential.
