PURPOSE
It contains the activities, outputs, and experiences that have become part of my learning throughout this academic journey. Each work represents not only the tasks I have completed but also the lessons I have learned, the challenges I have faced, and the improvements I have made along the way.
In this portfolio, I have several goals. First, I want to organize and showcase my academic works to keep track of my learning progress. Second, I want to reflect on my strengths and weaknesses so I can identify the areas that need improvement. Third, I aim to demonstrate the knowledge and skills I have developed, especially in communication, creativity, technology, and teaching. Lastly, I want this portfolio to serve as a reminder of my experiences and as a reference for my future teaching journey.
I hope to see not just a collection of completed requirements but also evidence of my growth as a student and as a future educator. I know that I still have a lot to learn, but I believe that every experience brings me one step closer to becoming the teacher I aspire to be. I created this portfolio to document my journey as a Bachelor of Secondary Education major in English.
